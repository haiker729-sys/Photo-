Production Architecture

Engineered for Professional Event Studios

Every feature below is implemented and verified inside the Jummo Photos AI Android application.

📷

Professional Camera

Full-screen CameraX viewfinder with Flash modes (Auto/On/Off), HDR toggle, 4K framing badge, rule-of-thirds composition grid, front/rear lens switching, and floating active event selector.

💾

DSLR / RAW Import

Zero-permission Android Photo Picker and Storage Access Framework (SAF) to import RAW, DNG, CR2, NEF, ARW, PNG, and JPG files directly from DSLR SD cards or camera storage.

☁️

Automatic Photo Upload

Resilient background upload queue powered by Android WorkManager (`PhotoUploadWorker`) with configurable Wi-Fi Only and Battery > 20% protection.

📁

Google Drive Integration

Connect your studio’s root Drive folder (`Jummo_Events_Cloud`) via a server-side OAuth architecture that keeps client secrets out of the Android APK.

🔳

Event QR Generation

Deterministic branded QR code generator with custom event title, category, expiration date, instant pause/resume switch, gallery download, and native Android Share sheet.

👤

AI Face Search

Consent-first guest selfie matching strictly isolated per Event ID. Guests can report mismatches or purge their face-search session data at any time.

🖼️

Guest Photo Gallery

3-column event gallery with All Photos, People (AI), and Highlights tabs, plus configurable guest access modes: Full Gallery, Face Search Only, or Photographer Approval.

🔒

Secure Photo Download

Original captured photos are stored safely in local app storage (`filesDir/photos`) and never auto-deleted. Guest downloads respect per-event authorization rules.

End-to-End Studio Workflow

How Jummo Photos AI Works

From live event capture to instant QR guest delivery in six streamlined steps.

STEP 01

1. Download the Android App

Download and install the signed Jummo Photos AI APK from the official GitHub Releases page on your Android phone or tablet.

STEP 02

2. Sign in with Google

Authenticate your studio workspace in one tap using Google Sign-In powered by Jetpack Credential Manager and Firebase Authentication.

STEP 03

3. Create an Event

Set the event name, category (Wedding, Birthday, Festival, School, Corporate), date, venue, cover photograph, and guest access rules.

STEP 04

4. Capture or Import Photos

Shoot directly with the built-in CameraX viewfinder or import RAW/JPG photos from your DSLR SD card. Originals remain safe on your device.

STEP 05

5. Generate Event QR

Open QR Management for any event to generate, download, or share a branded QR card with custom expiration and live pause controls.

STEP 06

6. Let Guests Find Authorized Photos

Guests scan the event QR code, provide explicit face-search consent, and view or download their authorized event photos.

In Profile & Settings → Google Drive, enable the Drive connection and specify your root cloud folder name (default: Jummo_Events_Cloud). The architecture uses a server-side OAuth bridge so no sensitive OAuth client secret is ever hardcoded inside the Android APK.
